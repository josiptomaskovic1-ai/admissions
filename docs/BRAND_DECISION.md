# Brand decision: The Candidate Studio

Decision date: 2026-09-26  
Status: selected for the private preview; domain and trade mark not yet registered.

## Selected identity

- **Name:** The Candidate Studio
- **Category descriptor:** Independent admissions advisory
- **Primary line:** Clarity for every step.
- **Public domain candidate:** `thecandidatestudio.com`

The name uses two familiar international words. “Candidate” has a recognizable cognate across Croatian, Bosnian, Serbian/Montenegrin, German and French; “studio” communicates close, considered work rather than a high-volume recruitment agency. The name describes the client at the centre of the process without making outcome, ranking or partnership claims.

The homepage audience line is service-led — “European university applications · guidance in your language” — rather than geography-led. “Balkans and diaspora” remains useful for SEO, advertising audiences and internal positioning, but is not the main promise.

## Preliminary screening

Public web searches on 2026-09-26 did not surface an exact admissions or education consultancy operating as “The Candidate Studio.” Verisign RDAP returned no `.com` domain object for `thecandidatestudio.com` at the time of checking, which normally indicates that the domain was not registered at that moment. Availability can change at any time and the domain has not been reserved by this repository.

This is a preliminary commercial screen, not legal clearance. Before a public launch or domain purchase, check EUIPO, WIPO and the relevant national registers, particularly education and consultancy classes, and have the final identity reviewed in the operator’s jurisdictions.

## Names not used

- **Virela:** memorable but semantically empty and too close to the common pattern of invented vowel-heavy agency names.
- **Meridian Admissions:** rejected because active admissions consultancies already use the exact name.
- **Applicant Studio:** rejected because `applicantstudio.com` is registered and the admissions market already contains close “Applicant Lab” and “Application Lab” brands.
- **Merit House:** meaningful, but the exact phrase is already used by active financial-services and education organisations, making search ownership weaker.

## Brand usage

Use the full name **The Candidate Studio** in the logo, metadata, legal/service information and first reference. Use **Candidate Studio** only when space is constrained and the full name is already visible. Do not shorten the name to “TCS,” which is strongly associated with an unrelated global company.
