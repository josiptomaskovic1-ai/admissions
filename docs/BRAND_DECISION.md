# Brand decision: Adria Admissions

Decision date: 2026-09-26  
Status: selected for the private preview; used by the supplied Calendly, email and LinkedIn identities; domain and trade mark not yet verified.

## Selected identity

- **Name:** Adria Admissions
- **Category descriptor:** Study abroad advisory
- **Primary line:** Study abroad. Apply with a clear plan.
- **Brand mark:** the supplied white “A” on deep navy, redrawn as a responsive vector for the website

“Adria” is short, pronounceable across the target Balkan markets and recognizable internationally without tying the service to one country. “Admissions” makes the category immediately clear. The combination is more direct and searchable than an invented name while avoiding claims about outcomes, rankings or university partnerships.

The homepage audience line is service-led — “European university applications · guidance in your language” — rather than geography-led. “Balkans and diaspora” remains useful for SEO, advertising audiences and internal positioning, but is not the main promise.

## Preliminary screening

The name is already used in the public identities supplied for this project. That establishes consistency, not legal clearance or exclusive availability.

This is a preliminary commercial screen, not legal clearance. Before a public launch or domain purchase, check EUIPO, WIPO and the relevant national registers, particularly education and consultancy classes, and have the final identity reviewed in the operator’s jurisdictions.

## Names not used

- **Virela:** memorable but semantically empty and too close to the common pattern of invented vowel-heavy agency names.
- **Meridian Admissions:** rejected because active admissions consultancies already use the exact name.
- **Applicant Studio:** rejected because `applicantstudio.com` is registered and the admissions market already contains close “Applicant Lab” and “Application Lab” brands.
- **Merit House:** meaningful, but the exact phrase is already used by active financial-services and education organisations, making search ownership weaker.

## Brand usage

Use the full name **Adria Admissions** in the logo, metadata, legal/service information and every first reference. The stylized “A” may stand alone only where the full name is visible nearby or space is constrained, such as the favicon.
